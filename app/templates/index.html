What the observations show

This grid cell contains repeated satellite-observed hotspot detections across multiple years. MODIS observations are concentrated in January, February and December. VIIRS also records substantial activity in these months, alongside detections throughout the rest of the year.

MODIS and VIIRS counts are shown separately because their observation periods, spatial resolutions, coverage and detection characteristics differ. These are observations, not unique fires or burned area. The grid cell does not represent a precise fire boundary or a specific fire cause.

INTERPRETATION & LIMITATIONS

Detection counts are not unique fire incidents

MODIS and VIIRS differ in spatial resolution, observation characteristics and operational periods. Their raw detection counts should not be interpreted as directly equivalent measures of fire occurrence or sensor performance. VIIRS observations begin in 2012, while MODIS observations extend back to 2000.

METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Historical patterns are not fire predictions

Monthly percentages represent the share of each sensor-family's annual detection observations, averaged across complete historical years. MODIS and VIIRS are kept separate because their sensor characteristics and coverage differ. Partial years 2000 and 2026 are excluded from the historical averages. Near-real-time observations are excluded. These patterns do not represent unique fires, burned area, fire causes, or predictions.

SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Persistence does not mean continuous burning

Active months represent distinct calendar months containing at least one detection in a grid cell. Active years represent distinct calendar years containing detections. These values do not indicate continuous fire duration or the number of unique fire incidents. Satellite observations may include repeated detections of the same event.

SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Concentration is not the same as fire intensity

Detection concentration describes the number of satellite detection observations assigned to each grid cell. Detection density divides this count by the approximate spherical area of that cell. Cell area varies with latitude. Average and maximum FRP describe the fire-radiative-power values recorded in the observations; they are not measurements of total burned area. Repeated observations may relate to the same fire. These results do not represent unique fires, fire boundaries, fire causes or continuous burning.
